# IVORQ v0.5 Cost Control Architecture Assessment

**Date:** 2026-06-19  
**Reviewer:** Hospitality ERP Architect / CTO Governance Reviewer

## 1. Executive Summary
Operating in ARCHITECTURE REVIEW MODE, this assessment evaluates the readiness of IVORQ's Cost Control foundation prior to the v0.5 roadmap phase. The system demonstrates advanced, production-grade capabilities for Goods Received Not Invoiced (GRNI), AVCO/WAC calculation, and Budget Enforcement. However, a critical gap exists in the **Consumption Costing flow**: while Inventory issues correctly capture the weighted average cost (WAC) locally, this cost is never posted to the General Ledger. The system operates as a "leaky bucket" where inventory value is consumed operationally but never reflected financially.

## 2. Current Cost Control Architecture
The Cost Control architecture spans the Purchasing, Inventory, and General Ledger domains. It relies on a highly decoupled event-driven model where operational actions (Receipts, Variances) emit events caught by Finance listeners to enforce ADR-004 (Finance Module Boundary).

### Trace Analysis: Procure-to-Pay & Consume
- **Purchase Request:** ✔️ Enforces budget constraints (`BudgetVarianceService`) and routes via `ApprovalEngineService`.
- **Purchase Order:** ✔️ Converted directly from approved PRs.
- **Goods Receipt:** ✔️ Triggers stock movement, updates WAC, and fires `InventoryReceiptPosted`.
- **Inventory Ledger:** ✔️ Logs physical quantities via `InventoryStockCard`.
- **Consumption:** ⚠️ Logs operational consumption via `IssueService`, stamping current WAC.
- **Cost Ledger (Consumption):** ❌ **BROKEN FLOW.** No event listeners broadcast the consumption to the GL. Cost is lost financially.
- **Cost Ledger (GRNI):** ✔️ `GrniPostingEngine` correctly creates liability accruals.
- **General Ledger:** ✔️ `JournalCandidateReevaluationService` actively manages unposted journals.

## 3. Inventory Ledger Assessment
- **Status:** **Ready**
- **Assessment:** Physical quantity tracking is strictly enforced via `InventoryStockCard`. Moving average calculations are isolated inside the `ReceiptService` with strict concurrency locks to prevent race conditions during bulk receiving.

## 4. Cost Ledger Assessment
- **Status:** **Partially Ready**
- **Assessment:** The General Ledger successfully accepts GRNI and Variance postings via dedicated engines (`GrniPostingEngine`, `VariancePostingEngine`). The subledger framework is robust but lacks the engine to process everyday inventory consumption.

## 5. GRNI Assessment
- **Status:** **Highly Advanced**
- **Assessment:** The architecture accurately separates the physical receipt of goods from the financial invoice (`OperationalIdentityEnum::GRNI_ACCRUAL` and `GRNI_RECEIPT`). `ApPostingEngine` natively handles GRNI-matched invoices, fulfilling enterprise AP requirements.

## 6. AVCO Assessment
- **Status:** **Ready**
- **Assessment:** The weighted average cost (WAC) formula is mathematically correct and updates dynamically upon Goods Receipt. Prior WAC contamination risks were resolved by enforcing strict `property_id` scoping inside `InventoryStockRepository`.

## 7. Variance Assessment
- **Status:** **Ready**
- **Assessment:** `VariancePostingListener` and `VariancePostingEngine` actively listen for discrepancies (e.g., Stock Count adjustments) and post them to the GL, ensuring physical vs. financial alignment.

## 8. Month-End Closing Assessment
- **Status:** **Skeleton Only**
- **Assessment:** `PeriodControlService` successfully acts as a time-lock, preventing transactions in closed periods. However, it lacks substantive month-end cost reconciliation routines (e.g., GRNI aging reports, un-invoiced receipt clearance, stock ledger vs. GL reconciliation).

## 9. Architecture Gaps
1. **Missing Consumption Posting Engine:** `IssueService` (Consumption) does not trigger GL postings. Inventory Asset accounts will falsely inflate while Departmental Expense accounts remain at zero.
2. **Missing Month-End Reconciliation:** No automated processes exist to reconcile the `InventoryStockCard` total monetary value against the General Ledger `LedgerBalance` for the Inventory Asset account.

## 10. Risk Assessment
- **Consumption Leakage (P0 Critical):** If multi-property operations begin with this gap, the entire Profit & Loss (P&L) statement will be inaccurate. Food & Beverage Cost of Sales will report 0% while inventory assets inflate indefinitely.
- **GRNI Aging Drift (P2 Medium):** Without a month-end GRNI clearing process, old un-invoiced receipts may clutter the liability accounts permanently.

## 11. Recommended Priorities
1. **[P0] Consumption Posting Engine:** Implement `ConsumptionPostingListener` and `ConsumptionPostingEngine` to credit Inventory Asset and debit Cost of Sales/Department Expense upon `IssueService` execution.
2. **[P1] Month-End Reconciliation:** Develop a closing routine that flags discrepancies between the physical stock valuation and the GL asset valuation.
3. **[P2] GRNI Aging Reports:** Build views to track aged receipts pending AP invoices.

## 12. Suggested Sprint Sequence (v0.5 Roadmap)
- **Sprint 14.9.1:** Consumption Financial Integration (Build `ConsumptionPostingEngine`).
- **Sprint 14.9.2:** Vendor Ownership Resolution (Execute ADR-006 schema shifts).
- **Sprint 14.9.3:** Month-End Closing & Reconciliation Tools.
- **Sprint 14.9.4:** Cost Control Reporting (P&L Cost of Sales, GRNI Aging).
